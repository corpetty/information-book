Act VI · Outlast

What holds them up — three rules

RULE 1
Monastery money

fund it outside the attention market: “revenue that depends on present attention is structurally incompatible with the integration project.” Existence proof: Wikipedia, donor-funded, holding. Live negative control: Stack Overflow, ad-pressured, decaying exactly on schedule.

RULE 2
Receipts for skeptics

“design for institutions that work when trust is partial, not for institutions that require trust to be complete.” Edit histories anyone — including your worst skeptic — can audit.

RULE 3
Own your tutor

a university training its own AI on its own vetted library, rewarded for being right, not agreeable. Never rent it from the machine it’s meant to counterbalance.
