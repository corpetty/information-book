*Mechanism · part of [[the-medium|The medium sets the rules]]*

<!-- card -->
Think of how anyone gets deep into a subject, whether chess, cooking or economics. They want to know a little, so they read a little. That gives them some vocabulary, which makes the next piece easier and more interesting, so they want more. If the wanting stops, the loop stops: the background fades, or never forms.

The book calls this **want as the prime mover**. It came out of walking back an early draft that ranked two mechanisms against each other: [[transport|transport]], the loss in every retelling, and [[selection|selection]], the choice of what gets passed on. Both turn out to sit downstream of wanting. How much a person can take in, their [[receiver-budget|budget of attention and background]], grows with past engagement. What they choose to pass on is shaped by the same history. The book now treats the two as parallel and co-equal, entangled through this one loop.

Two things follow. The loop supports the book's working answer that the budget is [[budget-fixed-or-trainable|trainable, not fixed]]. And it raises the matter of what feeds the wanting. The book's answer is that [[medium-shapes-want|the medium shapes what people want]], and so shapes the people who will judge the next round of ideas.
