*Mechanism · part of [[the-medium|The medium sets the rules]]*

<!-- card -->
A feed that rewards quick reactions trains you, over thousands of repetitions, to want quick things. A medium that rewards sustained attention, such as long-form writing, runs the loop the other way. The book calls this **medium shapes want**.

It builds on the claim that [[want-as-prime-mover|wanting drives everything else]]: people engage with what they want, and engaging builds the background that deepens the want. A medium feeds that loop. It filters today's content, and it edits the audience who will judge tomorrow's. This is the book's version of McLuhan's "the medium is the message", and one case of [[framework-pricing|a structure making some things cheap and others costly]].

The book hedges. Research it cites finds that people hostile online are also hostile offline, so a medium does not write wants from nothing. Over a year it acts like a casting director, choosing which old appetites get the megaphone. Over a generation it may act as a co-writer, a claim the book says it has not earned. Like other frameworks, it [[influence-not-imprisonment|biases without caging]]. An [[engagement-equilibrium|engagement-tuned feed]] runs the loop toward the shallow, which is why the book doubts that better content alone is a fix.
