*Mechanism · part of [[how-ideas-travel|How ideas travel]]*

<!-- card -->
Open any feed and watch yourself for a minute. You scroll past a hundred things and stop on one. It is almost never the most accurate thing on the screen. It made you feel something, or said something about your side, or was easy. Multiply that split-second choice by everyone, all day, and you have what the book calls **selection**.

At every stage of an idea's journey there is a gate: a funder, a journal reviewer, an editor, a ranking algorithm, a person deciding whether to share. Each passes a little and drops most, by its own local test. The book calls what those tests reward [[memetic-fitness|memetic fitness]], and truth is not on the list.

Selection runs alongside [[transport|transport]], the loss in every retelling, but differs in kind. Transport is a blind cost. Selection is a choice made by criteria, and criteria can be set, tuned and captured. That is why the book centers on it: selection is where [[manipulation-surface|manipulation]] acts and where any repair would have to act. The slowest and least visible gate is the medium, which decides in advance [[option-space|which forms an idea can take]], much as any [[framework|framework]] shapes the thoughts built inside it.
