*Mechanism · part of [[made-by-agreement|Truths made by agreement]]*

<!-- card -->
A rumor says the bank is failing. Depositors hear it and pull their money out, and the bank fails. Nothing was distorted along the way. The spreading was the event.

The book calls this constitutive transmission. For an ordinary fact, the chain of retellings works like a security camera: it can film badly, but it cannot change what happened. For [[truth-generators|truths made by agreement]] (money, law, nations) the camera is part of the scene. Each person who takes the claim in becomes one more holder of the agreement. So reach changes the truth itself: the billionth holder of a currency adds to its value. Its sibling for facts is [[transport|transport]].

This is the book's own version of [[performativity|performativity]], where a financial model reshapes the market it describes (see [[black-scholes|Black-Scholes]]), and a case of [[framework-feedback|framework feedback]]. It leads to the [[constitutive-regimes|three states an agreement can be in]], and to a warning about speed. The 2007 run on Northern Rock moved at the pace of pavement queues. In 2023 Silicon Valley Bank lost tens of billions in deposits in one day. The book adds a hedge: one person's share may be nearly notional in calm times and decisive only in a crisis.
