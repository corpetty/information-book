*Mechanism · part of [[maps-and-territories|When the map moves the territory]]*

<!-- card -->
Suppose a city ranks its schools by test scores. Within a few years the schools have changed: more test preparation, less of everything the test does not cover. The scores rise. Whether the children know more is now hard to tell, because the thing that was supposed to tell you has been reshaped by being used.

**Framework feedback** is the loop in which a [[framework]]'s outputs get acted on and the world shifts in response. It can run toward the framework: foresters planted the tidy forest their tables described ([[scientific-forestry]]), and traders priced options by a formula until prices fit it ([[performativity]]). It can run away from it: a measure chased as a target stops measuring ([[goodharts-law|Goodhart's law]]), and people sorted into a category change, which changes the category ([[looping-effects|looping effects]]).

The book argues this is where a framework's bias does its real damage. A biased map misleads its reader. A map that is acted on rebuilds the ground, and the mismatch that would have exposed it disappears. It is the general form of what the book calls [[constitutive-transmission|constitutive transmission]].
