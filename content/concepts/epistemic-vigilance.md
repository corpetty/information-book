> **Concept**

The Sperberian-tradition construct for the cognitive defenses humans have evolved to screen incoming information for plausibility and source reliability — 'mental immune systems' in the popular framing. Mercier extends and refines the term as *open vigilance* (see open-vigilance concept) to mark that the system is paired with openness rather than being a one-sided defense layer over a credulous default. The catalog keeps both entries because earlier book chapters used 'epistemic vigilance' before the Mercier extraction sharpened it.
