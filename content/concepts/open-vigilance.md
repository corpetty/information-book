> **Concept**

Mercier's refinement of Sperber's epistemic-vigilance term. The system is paired: humans are simultaneously *open to* useful information and *vigilant against* unreliable sources, with the two faces evolving hand-in-hand as communication became broader and more powerful. The earlier 'epistemic vigilance' label flattens this into a one-sided defense construct, which is what the catalog had until the Mercier extraction surfaced the paired reading. Two named sub-mechanisms: *plausibility checking* (does the message fit pre-existing beliefs?) and *reasoning* (do supporting arguments resonate?). Calibration is asymmetric: more errors of omission (not trusting when we should) than of commission (trusting when we shouldn't) — vigilance is conservative-leaning, not credulous-leaning.
