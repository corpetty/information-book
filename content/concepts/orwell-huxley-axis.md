> **Concept**

Postman's framing of two opposite failure modes for an information ecosystem. The Orwell mode: control by inflicted pain — censorship, surveillance, information withheld. The Huxley mode: control by inflicted pleasure — truth not concealed but drowned in a sea of engaging irrelevance. The book's diagnosis sits squarely in the Huxley mode: the modern failure is abundance optimized for engagement, not scarcity enforced by a censor.
