> **Concept**

What 5c's truth-regimes become when the cargo's generator is the agreement itself: convergent constitution (decodes compose into one determinate agreement — most money and law, most of the time), divergent constitution (decodes pool into camps affirming the same words as different agreements — segmentation read one level up, the truth coming apart), and hollow constitution (symbolic affirmation detaches from the constituting behavior — everyone still calls it money, nobody holds it). The failure axis rotates from error to spread-and-substance: an intersubjective truth cannot be inverted, but it can split or empty. Carries the same two-plus-one suspicion as 5c's regimes, inherited knowingly.
