> **Concept**

A self-reinforcing loop formalized by O'Connor & Weatherall: when agents discount evidence from sources they distrust, communities that begin with mild disagreement diverge — each side discounts the other's findings, beliefs pull apart, and the divergence deepens the distrust that drove it. Polarization here needs no irrationality and no misinformation; rational trust-weighting plus an initial difference is enough.
