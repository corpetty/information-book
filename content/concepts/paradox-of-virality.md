> **Concept**

What spreads is not what people endorse. Across the political spectrum, people report they do not want high-arousal negative content to go viral — yet it does. The gap implicates the selection gate (especially algorithmic amplification), not receiver preference. (Catalog gap flagged by the psychology-of-virality extraction.)
