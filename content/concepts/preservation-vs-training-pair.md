> **Concept**

What 'bureaucracy' was bundling: preservation keeps the un-compressed form alive somewhere; training re-installs the capacity to decode at that resolution in receivers. Each can be done by many institutional forms (monasteries, journals, universities, common-law courts, version-controlled specs) and each fails differently when starved, captured, or displaced. The pair acts as a pump — preservation holds pressure, training releases it into the network — so either alone fails the integration project.
