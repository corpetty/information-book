> **Concept**

Postman's distinction between a technology — the machine or tool itself — and a medium — the social and intellectual environment that grows up around how the technology is used. The book's 'medium' always means the latter: not the device but the environment of capacities, gate-criteria, and cultivated wants it produces. The same technology can host different media, which guards against blaming the hardware for what is really a pattern of use.
