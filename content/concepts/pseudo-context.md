> **Concept**

Postman's term for a structure invented to give decontextualized information the appearance of a use it does not really have. The information may be true and accurately measured, but it has been stripped of the context that connected it to action; pseudo-context — the news quiz, trivia, conversation-fodder — papers over the gap. Distinct from manufactured content, which never measured anything: pseudo-context is real information made idle.
