> **Concept**

The carving that disciplines the three-realities frame: objective truths are generated by reality (the pipeline can garble the claim but cannot touch the fact); intersubjective truths are generated by the network's agreement — kept as truths, in Harari's terms, because the generator differs, not the truth-ness. Carves per-claim, not per-artifact: a myth can be based in reality too, and mixed bundles (founding stories, astrology) split along the generator line. Every intersubjective truth also casts an objective shadow — that the agreement holds is an ordinary objective fact about the network — and keeping a truth apart from its shadow is what keeps reporting-on-a-constitution distinct from participating-in-one.
