*Concept · part of [[made-by-agreement|Truths made by agreement]]*

<!-- card -->
Take two true sentences: the Earth goes around the Sun, and a twenty-dollar bill buys groceries. Now wipe every belief about each from every head. The Earth keeps orbiting, and anyone with a telescope can find the fact again. The bill becomes ink on paper. Nothing is left to re-measure, because the truth was the agreement.

The book says both sentences are true and differ in their **generator**. Reality generates the first. A network's agreement generates the second. This sharpens Harari's [[three-realities|three realities]], and it sorts claims one at a time, because most things are mixed bundles. A nation's founding story holds checkable battles and dates alongside the nation itself, which exists by agreement. [[astrology|Astrology]] bundles false claims about the planets with a real shared practice.

Every agreement also casts an "objective shadow." That most shops accept dollars is a plain fact a survey can check, and checking it never touches the agreement itself. Mistaking the agreement for a fact of nature is what the book calls [[reification|reification]]. The generator idea also supports the claim that [[constitutive-transmission|spreading an agreement is taking part in it]]. The book admits the line is unsettled in hard cases, scientific consensus among them.
