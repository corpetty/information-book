> **Concept**

The fracturing of a network into sub-groups along the seams of its binding myth's interpretive latitude. The segments affirm the same myth while decoding it differently, so the network is superficially unified and structurally fragmented at once. Distinct from echo chambers vs. bubbles: this is the process that produces those structures.
