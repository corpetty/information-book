> **Concept**

Deep specialist expertise paired with metacognitive flexibility — the second-order capacity to recognize structural analogies across domains, suspend one's primary paradigm, and treat one's own frame as one option rather than the substrate of reality. The bridge-node mechanism: depth is what gets a bridge admitted as a peer on either side; flexibility is what lets it see and translate the structural analogy that crosses.
