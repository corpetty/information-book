> **Open question**

The book's diagnostic vocabulary (true / false / corrective / manufactured) was built for objective claims, where the pipeline transmits-or-distorts a fact it cannot touch. Intersubjective truths (currencies, nations, laws, brands) are generated by network agreement, not by reality — the pipeline partly constitutes them. Ch 2 names the distinction and promises a fuller working-through; 5c and Ch 9 carry explicit scope debts against it; the outline lists pull-ins for 5b/8/10/11. But the book has no worked machinery for constitution: no regime model, no account of constitutive capture, no corrective analog for content with no external check.
