*Open question · part of [[made-by-agreement|Truths made by agreement]]*

<!-- card -->
When a lab result is mangled into a bad headline, the result itself is unharmed, and someone can run the experiment again. Now take another kind of truth: a twenty-dollar bill buys groceries. Mangle that one and there is nothing to re-run, because the truth was the agreement.

The book's tools were built for the first. The open question was what the chain from source to audience does to the second kind, the "intersubjective" third of Harari's [[three-realities|three realities]].

Its working answer: these are real truths, [[truth-generators|generated by agreement]], and [[constitutive-transmission|passing them on is taking part in them]]. They cannot be wrong, though they can split or go hollow (the [[constitutive-regimes|constitutive regimes]]). Capture means steering whose reading counts, under cover of [[reification|reification]]. With no outside referee, a failing agreement gets renegotiated, abandoned or forked, as when Ethereum split into two ledgers in 2016. A captured chain distorts a fact. It decides an agreement.

The book marks this provisionally resolved. Still open: where the line between the two kinds falls (scientific consensus is the hard case), whether three regimes is one too many, and how the account squares with earlier work it has not yet engaged, such as Hacking's [[looping-effects|looping effects]].

> [!note]- The working answer, in the author's words
> Worked out in intersubjective-truth.md. Intersubjective truths are truths (Harari's terms kept) whose generator is the network's agreement rather than reality, carved per-claim, not per-artifact — a myth can be based in reality too. The pipeline is wired into the generator: transmission is participation in constitution, reach changes the truth itself, and no unharmed fact waits outside a bad transit. The transmission regimes translate to convergent / divergent / hollow constitution (failure axis: spread-and-substance, not error); capture becomes steering the generator — re-weighting whose decodes count — camouflaged by reification; the corrective's role passes to the fork (schism, secession, dollarization, chain split), which determines how many truths there are rather than who was right; and the transport collapse cuts twice — its second blade is constitutive volatility, agreement states moving at meme speed against maintenance institutions built for committee speed. Threaded through Chs 2, 5, 5b, 5c, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12. Still open: the line between generators (consensus-tracks vs. agreement-generates), the regime translation's two-plus-one suspicion, the volatility ratio claim, the sibling treatment's worked cases, and engagement with the social-ontology prior art (Searle, Berger & Luckmann, Anderson, Hacking, Soros, Hirschman).
