Original prescription was 'multiple smaller networks.' Critique notes small networks have their own compression dynamics and tend toward echo chambers. The hard problem is moving complex truth between networks that don't share preconditions.

**Status.** provisionally resolved

**Where the book lands.** Integration between networks that don't share preconditions, not fragmentation into smaller ones. Bridge nodes carrying transferable preconditions are the stitching infrastructure.
