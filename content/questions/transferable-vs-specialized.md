*Open question · part of [[building-bridges|Building bridges that last]]*

<!-- card -->
Suppose you want someone to carry a physics result to economists. The obvious candidate is a generalist with all-purpose skills: statistical literacy, careful reading, the habit of asking for evidence. The book calls these [[transferable-preconditions|transferable preconditions]], and its first guess was that they make the bridge. The open question was whether training should tilt toward them or toward specialization.

The book's answer is that the choice was framed wrong. General skills do not let you read a particle-physics paper: an expert sees whole patterns where you read sentence by sentence and run out of working memory. And the receiving community treats a generalist as a low-credibility outsider, which is [[polarization-via-distrust|polarization via distrust]] at work.

What crosses is [[versatile-expertise|versatile expertise]]: a deep specialist who has added a second skill, the habit of looking for structural analogies and of [[framework-rotation|treating their own paradigm as one option among several]]. Depth earns admission as a peer. Flexibility does the translating, and it disarms the [[curse-of-expertise|curse of expertise]]. Depth that has looked into [[abyss|the abyss]] of its own field corrects itself.

The book marks this provisionally resolved. Its hedges: such people are slow to produce, and some fields do useful work because they are isolated.

**Status:** provisionally resolved.

> [!note]- The working answer, in the author's words
> The dichotomy was wrong. A bridge node between specialist communities is neither a generalist with field-general preconditions nor a pure specialist, but a deep specialist who has paired their depth with metacognitive flexibility — what double-edged-sword-of-expertise calls versatile expertise. Depth is what gets the bridge admitted as a peer on either side; metacognitive flexibility (structural-analogy habits, paradigm suspension, intellectual humility) is what lets the bridge translate across, and is also what disarms the curse-of-expertise failure modes that are the cognitive substrate of polarization-via-distrust. The book's prescription tilts toward cultivating that paired skill inside specialist communities, not toward generalist training. Drafted in bridge-nodes-and-versatile-expertise.md.
