Start here if you are new. This is the basic machine the rest of the book runs on: a chain of stages from the world to your phone, a loss at every hop, a filter at every hop, and a difference between the two that turns out to matter more than anything else.

## The ideas

Each one is a short page. Walk them in this order, or start with whichever catches your eye.

1. [[transport|Transport]] *(mechanism)*: Each retelling of an idea for a new audience loses something, and what goes first is usually the fine print on how to read it.
2. [[telephone-game|The telephone game]] *(example)*: Whisper a sentence around a circle of ten children and it comes out garbled, which shows how retelling alone wears an idea down.
3. [[selection|Selection]] *(mechanism)*: At every step, someone or something decides which ideas get passed on, and that decision follows rules that people can set on purpose.
4. [[memetic-fitness|Memetic fitness]]: What gets passed along is whatever stirs feeling, flatters a group, or gives people something to do, and being true is not on that list.
5. [[option-space|Option space]]: Before anyone chooses which version of an idea to pass on, the format has already decided which versions can exist at all.
6. [[manufactured-injection|Manufactured injection]] *(mechanism)*: Some of what circulates as information was never checked against the world at all; it was made up and dropped into the stream partway down.
7. [[astrology|Astrology]] *(example)*: Horoscopes use real star and planet positions, but the idea that those positions shape your personality was made up and never tested.
8. [[scientists-say-x|'Scientists say X' headline]] *(example)*: Twenty years of careful research can reach the public as one confident headline, with every caveat and condition left behind.
9. [[transport-vs-selection|Transport vs. selection: which is primary?]] *(open question)*: Ideas get worn down in retelling and also filtered by what people choose to pass on, and only the filtering can be steered.
10. [[three-layer-message|The three-layer message (frame / outer / inner)]]: Every message has three parts: a sign that it is a message, the know-how needed to read it, and the content itself.
11. [[voyager-golden-record|The Voyager Golden Record]] *(example)*: A record sent into deep space had to carry its own playing instructions, because nobody could assume the finder would know anything.
