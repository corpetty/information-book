A new kind of link in the chain, and the first to own almost every setting at once. The hopeful case and the grim case are the same technology under different ownership.

**The same ground, told as a story:** [[ai-as-new-node|Chapter 11: AI as a New Kind of Node]].

## The ideas

Each one is a short page. Walk them in this order, or start with whichever catches your eye.

1. [[llm-design-moment-collapse|LLM as collapsed selection-design moment]]: With an AI chatbot, one company picks what it learned from, what it was rewarded for, how it is configured, and also writes the answer.
2. [[decompression-on-demand|Decompression on demand]]: An AI can unpack a dense paper into an explanation fitted to what you already know, as long as it stays faithful to the source.
3. [[compressed-form-as-authority|Compressed form becoming authoritative]]: When an AI summary is treated as the answer, people stop reading the original, and the summary quietly takes its place.
4. [[corpus-capture|Corpus capture]]: An AI trained on a lopsided selection of text gives lopsided answers, however carefully it is handled afterward.
5. [[objective-capture|Objective capture]]: An AI rewarded for pleasing people learns to please rather than to be right, and its output then feeds the next AI's training.
6. [[deployment-capture|Deployment capture]]: The company running an AI can change how it answers with hidden instructions, without retraining it and without users seeing the change.
7. [[llm-as-capability-extender|LLM as capability extender (inside substrate custody)]]: A university that trains and runs its own AI on its own vetted library could teach far more people, with real experts checking the output.
