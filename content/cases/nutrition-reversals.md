Fat was the problem. Then low-fat was the answer. Then carbohydrates, then sugar, then ultra-processed food. Anyone who has followed diet news for a few decades has lived through the whole sequence.

Through all of it the field's position was closer to "it is complicated, and the studies are confounded." Each turn in the public story was a real finding with its hedges and conditions removed, sold as one clean line you could act on. The whiplash is produced in the retelling, and the audience ends up with a working model of nutrition that is worse than having none.

It is a case of the [[bridge-zone|bridge zone]] at work. A [[compressed-form|compressed version]] travels and the qualifications stay behind, because a single cause is easier to pass on than a tangle ([[handle-ability]], and [[memetic-fitness|what the filters reward]]). The book adds a caution against a tidy reading: the specialist consensus was itself contested, and food-industry conflicts of interest run through this field. The run of reversals also shows how [[interpretive-latitude|a stripped-down claim can be read many ways]].
