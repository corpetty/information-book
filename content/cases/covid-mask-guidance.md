> **Case study**

That is a field updating on evidence. The bridge zone shipped it as two confident slogans, "masks don't work" then "masks work," with the decoding key (the supply motive, the provisional evidence) stripped, so the reversal read not as science working but as experts lying or flip-flopping. The inversion regime operating on institutional credibility itself, and the case that did the most public-trust damage.
