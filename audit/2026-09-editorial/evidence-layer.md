# Evidence-layer audit — *Lossy* (information-book)

Scope: `data/claims.json` (39 claims, 36 active), `data/sources.json` (29 sources, 7 extracted),
`data/interpretive/*.jsonl` (227 triples), `data/edges.jsonl` (built graph), the six Part III/IV
chapters, `content/experiments/`, `scripts/EXTRACTION_PROMPT.md`. Read-only; nothing in the repo
was modified. Dated 2026-09-17.

**Headline numbers.** 36 active claims; 20 have zero `supports`/`pressureTests` edges. 13 of those
20 carry a hand-authored `evidencedBy` pointer, but 7 of the 13 point at sources that are *already
extracted* (`misinformation-age`, `not-born-yesterday`, `psychology-of-virality`,
`amusing-ourselves-to-death`, `info-evolution-social-media`) — the claims were promoted after Phase 4
and never got a re-extraction pass. That is the cheapest fix in this audit. The other big structural
gap is the intersubjective-truth cluster (6 claims, 0 backing, 6 queued-but-unextracted sources).

Two mechanical notes before the substance:

- `data/extraction-catalog.json` is gitignored and regenerated by `make catalog`; any extraction
  run must be preceded by it or the six post-Phase-47 claims will not be valid object ids.
- `data/sources.json` has no `author`/`year` for the four open papers. For the record (from the
  PDFs' first pages): Wang & Wood 2011, *Applied Mathematical Modelling* 35; Adamic, Lento, Adar &
  Ng 2014 (arXiv 1402.6792; WSDM 2016) — actual title is **"Information Evolution in Social
  Networks"**, not "…Social Media"; Michael S. Jones, "Dawkins' Theory of Memetics – A Biological
  Assault on the Cultural" (undated in the PDF); Rathje & Van Bavel, "The Psychology of Virality",
  in press at *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* (2025).

## 4. Overstated or wrong factual claims (⚠)

- ⚠ `content/integration-problem.md:136` — Stack Overflow "decoupled-ish funding (originally
  subscription-style, increasingly ad-supported…)". **Wrong.** Stack Overflow was ad- and
  job-board-funded from launch (2008; Careers/Jobs from 2009), later Teams/Enterprise; there was
  never a subscription phase. The same error is restated at
  `content/infrastructure-for-integration.md:115` ("Decoupled from attention markets early in its
  history"). The case then "confirms" a principle it does not actually test.
- ⚠ `content/integration-problem.md:132` — Wikipedia "editor population is aging and not being
  replaced at scale". Overstated: active-editor counts fell 2007–2014 and have been roughly flat
  since; Halfaker et al. 2013 attribute the decline to newcomer rejection, not aging. Also
  "no attention-market revenue" ignores Wikimedia Enterprise (2021–), a paid API sold to Google et al.
- ⚠ `content/political-economy-of-attention.md:79` — mempool archive "over fifteen terabytes
  covering more than five billion transactions", "two firms build the large majority" of blocks,
  a provider "collects exactly that pairing". Plausible orders of magnitude but sourced solely to
  the author's own unextracted post; no primary is cited anywhere in the graph. Pin to primaries
  (builder-share dashboards, the provider's privacy policy, the archive vendor's own page) before
  publication.
- ⚠ `content/political-economy-of-attention.md:81` — "roughly three-quarters of new blocks, at the
  peak" censored sanctioned transactions (OFAC-compliant relays peaked ≈78% in Nov 2022 — right
  magnitude, uncited); "billions… since 2020", "seventy-two thousand instances" in one 30-day window
  — uncited. Same fix.
- ⚠ `content/political-economy-of-attention.md:109,149` — "most meme variants follow a Yule
  process: neutral drift". Mis-describes Adamic et al.: the Yule process there is a
  preferential-attachment model of the *variant-frequency distribution*, not a neutral-drift null,
  and the paper's headline result is that some n-grams carry a *replicative advantage* — i.e.
  selection. The "two refinements" the chapter takes on board rest on this reading. Also the source
  title is wrong in `sources.json` ("Social Media" → "Social Networks").
- ⚠ `content/emotional-memetics.md:29` — the Ukraine before/after finding is attributed to
  *Psychology of Virality*; it is a review citation of Kyrychenko et al., *Nature Communications*
  15:8127 (2024). Cite the primary.
- ⚠ `content/emotional-memetics.md:43` — "Mercier reframes Sperber's epistemic vigilance".
  Epistemic vigilance is Sperber, Clément, Heintz, Mascaro, **Mercier**, Origgi & Wilson (2010);
  Mercier is a co-author of the concept he is said to be reframing.
- ⚠ `content/preservation-vs-training.md:121` — "The Soviet sciences kept producing (preservation
  more or less intact) under a captured training apparatus". Lysenkoism (1948–64) captured
  *preservation* (genetics banned, Vavilov dead in prison); physics survived for bomb reasons. The
  Soviet case is a counter-example to the asymmetry as stated, not a supporting one.
- ⚠ `content/preservation-vs-training.md:91` — newsroom training side "has all but collapsed".
  US newsroom employment fell roughly by half 2008–2020 (Pew); "all but collapsed" is hyperbole.
  `political-economy-of-attention.md:97` "local journalism has collapsed" is defensible but
  uncited (Abernathy/Medill).
- ⚠ `content/political-economy-of-attention.md:37` — Postman's foreword "drawing on Huxley's

## 5. Experiments folder

`content/experiments/` holds one file, `measuring-interpretive-latitude.md` (status: open, logged
2026-05-21). It targets `compression-widens-latitude` and, through it, the dilution loop. It is
well-designed: latitude operationalised as *dispersion of implications conditional on endorsement*,
with false consensus as the discriminating fingerprint; a "cheapest first move" (diachronic
embeddings); named in-the-wild data (ANES/GSS symbolic-vs-operational; GitHub fork rates; LLM
sampling as instrument). Two additions it needs: (a) the Hamilton et al. 2016 law of conformity as
a confound — frequency alone slows semantic change, so the embedding study must control for
frequency or use contextual-variance methods (Giulianelli, Del Tredici & Fernández, "Analysing
Lexical Semantic Change with Contextualised Word Representations", ACL 2020); (b) Guilbeault et al.
2021 as the pre-registered adversary — if scale produces convergence in category tasks, the loop
needs to say why myths differ from categories.
