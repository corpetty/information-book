## 1. Argument

**Thesis.** Compression never touches a claim's truth; it strips the instructions for reading the claim, and what the reader substitutes decides whether the truth survives, flips, or stops being the point — and in engagement media the gate reliably picks the flipped or tribal version.

**Does it earn it?** Yes, in the middle third. L27–33 (truth lives in the key-fit), L39–45 (the three regimes) and L53–55 (transport scrambles, selection picks the regime) are a real argument with a real move. Around that core the chapter sags:

- The thesis is stated three times before the argument starts (L7, L15, L31) and twice after (L77, L79).
- L9–15 is 330 words of book-as-artifact ("close a hole", "the bill comes due") before the reader gets a single example.
- L41 is a 220-word hedge dropped into the middle of the regime exposition, then repeated at L86.
- L47 demotes the three-regime structure to "two-plus-one" the moment it's built, then repeats the demotion at L85. Either commit or say it once.
- L59–65 is a second essay (Harari's truth/order axis, three realities, reification) grafted on; L65 alone is ~330 words and imports two more notes.
- Terms the reader doesn't have yet: "want-loop" and "gate-criteria" (L57, from a side note), "frame message" (L69), "handle-ability" and "precondition-count" (L89), "Part IV" (L49, L81).

Move L41 into the uncertainty section (merge with L86) and pick one home for the two-plus-one doubt (L47 *or* L85). That removes ~600 words of throat-clearing, and the reader meets the coffee example (L29) within the first 200 words instead of the first 1,100.

**Rewrites (4 worst paragraphs).**

*L9–13 (original ~330 words):* "This chapter exists to close a hole … This chapter is where the bill comes due."
> **Rewrite (~110 words):** The machine described so far has no truth-meter. Transport re-encodes; selection decides what passes; neither mentions whether what arrives is true. So a fair reader could finish Chapter 5 and shrug: communication has always been lossy, and the short version is often the only version that reaches anyone. Nothing in the mechanism says anything broke. But "something has broken" is the sentence this book is bending toward, and it's a claim about truth. I can't get it from a mechanism that never refers to truth. This chapter is where I try.

*L41 (original ~220 words):* "I am going to use the words 'close,' 'roughly,' and 'approximately' repeatedly …"
> **Rewrite (~60 words, moved to uncertainties):** "Close" is doing work I can't yet measure. Heliocentrism survives any amount of compression; a regression coefficient dies the moment its confidence interval comes off. The difference is how much of the stripped key was holding the truth value up, and I don't have a threshold for that, or even a proof that it's a threshold rather than a slope.

*L57 (original ~200 words):* "This is where the chapter can pay a debt …"
> **Rewrite (~90 words):** The medium note said truth survives a medium under two conditions: the gate rewards something truth-correlated, and the medium doesn't erode the reader's appetite for truth. It couldn't say what "truth-correlated" meant. Now it can. The first condition is what keeps a claim in preservation instead of inversion: a gate that rewards correspondence picks the low-resolution-true variant off the menu. The second is what keeps the reader holding a truth-shaped key at all, instead of a pure identity key; that is the condition for not sliding into orthogonality. Two conditions, two failure regimes.

*L65 (original ~330 words):* "That relocation matters because it makes the axis *addressable* …"
> **Rewrite (~130 words):** If truth-versus-order is a property of a whole network's design, the only fix is "redesign the network," which is a sentence, not a plan. If order-over-truth is instead millions of per-gate choices between rewarding correspondence and rewarding coordination, there's a target: the gates. Harari's three realities give the same point an edge. Orthogonality is a claim migrating out of objective reality, where it had an answer, into intersubjective reality, where it's replaced by being jointly affirmed. Intersubjective reality is fine; money and law live there. The damage is an objective claim being processed as if it were intersubjective. The mirror case, an agreement processed as if it were physics, is reification, and [[intersubjective-truth]] handles it.
